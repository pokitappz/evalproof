Terms of service

Plans

Local diagnostics on developer machines are free. Running EvalProof in continuous integration, including the check gate and the GitHub Action, requires an active Team subscription for the organization that owns the repository.

One subscription, one organization

A Team subscription covers one organization and all of its repositories, projects and developers. Using one license key for additional organizations requires additional subscriptions.

Trials, billing and cancellation

New subscriptions include a 14-day free trial. Lemon Squeezy bills monthly as merchant of record. Cancel any time from the billing portal; access continues until the end of the paid period, and CI leases remain valid for up to seven days after they were issued.

Results

EvalProof reports evidence about your grader on the test population you define. It does not guarantee the accuracy of your AI system in production. The statistical guarantees and their assumptions are documented in the project's statistical policy.
